Not Just a Bar.

The Eight One Nine isn’t something you just walk into — and that’s by design. Good energy only. Great company required.

Named after our anniversary — August 19th — it started as a simple idea to redo a dining room.

It didn’t stay simple.

There’s a door. If you know, you know. And if it’s your first time, you sign it.

After that, you’re part of the story — whether you meant to be or not.

Do you have a Members Only card? Did you even know there was one?

The criteria exists, but it is not written down. Few know it. Even fewer qualify.

Tips

If you thought this was where we ask for a tip, it’s not that kind of bar.

Having you here is the good part. Sharing a drink and staying later than planned — that’s the whole point (unless "Closing Time" comes on, and then you stayed TOO long and it's time to leave lol).

We don’t even take a profit on the merch, so we’re definitely not passing around a tip jar.

Quality people are rare, and to us, that's exactly what you are.

So thanks for giving us your time and helping create new stories, some of which will no doubt become part of the 819 legend.
